Overview

64 apartments over a rooftop pool

Galleria Residences is a ground-plus-four building in Dubai South, the 145 km² master-planned district around Al Maktoum International Airport and next to Expo City. Each floor holds 16 apartments: 4 studios, 4 one-bedroom and 8 two-bedroom homes, all with private balconies and one parking space.

The rooftop is given over to residents, with an infinity pool, deck seating, a gym, sauna and steam rooms.
